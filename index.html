Welcome to Cali Weed

A Decade of Meticulous California Breeding

Ten years ago, we decided to reduce the “risk factor” when it came to supplying cannabis seeds. As a distributor and supplier, being wholly dependent on third-party growers meant quality control was never guaranteed.

We decided to invest in the long term and began a slow, meticulous breeding program in California—concentrating on developing the “best of the best” across select signature varieties. After six years of research, we began supplying seeds harvested exclusively from our own master plants.

The reality is that it’s not uncommon for the modern cannabis grower to have an extensive background in horticulture, commercial farming, or a formal degree in plant science. Every verified bag and seed pack carries our uncompromising California guarantee.
